How DealSleuth checks a deal

  1. Found, not fed. Every deal on this page was found by our agent in a live deals source on Nov 27, 2026 — the day Black Friday opened — and read at the store it names.
  2. Checked, with a time. Each card shows when its price was checked and the source it was read in. A price with no source is not a deal here.
  3. Scored, honestly. Deal Score = discount % + 10 when a source calls the price a lowest-ever, capped at 99. Verdicts: LOWEST EVER (a source says so), DEEP CUT 50%+, STRONG DROP 40–49%, SOLID DEAL 30–39%, TYPICAL SALE under 30%. A small discount on a big ticket can still save you more — sort by “Biggest saving ($)” to see those first.
  4. Compared across stores. Where the same product is cheaper elsewhere, the card says so and the cheapest store is marked.

Prices checked Nov 27, 2026, 06:20 UTC. “Was” prices are the retailer’s previous price as shown by the source. Prices can change; the store link on each card opens the product at its store.
